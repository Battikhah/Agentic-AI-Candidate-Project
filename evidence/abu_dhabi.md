# Abu Dhabi branch evidence

**Checked 2026-09-23.** Current public pages reviewed; delivery menus are platform listings and can change. **Fact** means directly supported below; **recommendation/assumption** is a proposal to validate. No rent, footfall, sales or popularity estimates are asserted.

## 1. Candidate areas and tradeoffs

- **Al Reem Island — recommendation for a first test:** the Abu Dhabi Residents Office describes high-rise waterfront living, young singles/couples, family amenities, schools and two malls. Government material confirms mixed residential, retail and commercial development. This supports a delivery-plus-takeaway hypothesis; likely higher occupancy costs and actual order density are **unverified**. [Residents Office area guide](https://www.adro.gov.ae/Living-in-Abu-Dhabi/Where-To-Live) · [Abu Dhabi Media Office](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/topic/al-reem-island/)
- **Khalifa City/Shakhbout City — family-oriented alternative:** the Residents Office cites villas, schools, nurseries, supermarkets and local eateries; test family bundles and car access. Lower-density geometry may constrain walk-in trade; compare unit-level delivery coverage, not presumed footfall. [Residents Office](https://www.adro.gov.ae/Living-in-Abu-Dhabi/Where-To-Live)
- **Al Khalidiyah — central alternative:** established apartments, schools, park, beach and mall; potentially useful for lunch/dinner mix. Relative rent, traffic and competitor density need broker quotes and on-site counts. [Residents Office](https://www.adro.gov.ae/Living-in-Abu-Dhabi/Where-To-Live)

## 2. Competitor categories, examples and position

**Direct competitors (overlapping products):** [Shawarma Planet](https://www.talabat.com/uae/restaurant/13803/shawarma-planet-al-maqta?aid=6485) lists shawarma pizza and wraps; [BBQ Shawarma](https://www.talabat.com/uae/restaurant/772220/bbqshawarma-al-khalidiyah?aid=6485) lists grilled kebab and shawarma sandwiches. **Indirect competitor:** Papa Johns is confirmed in Abu Dhabi by its [store locator](https://www.papajohns.ae/stores) (including Reem Mall and Khalifa City locations); its [UAE menu](https://www.papajohns.ae/menu) lists All The Meats. These listings establish presence and product overlap, not popularity or market share.

**Recommendation:** position as a Jordanian specialist: recognizably Jordanian meat-pizza and kebab sandwiches, consistent portions, fast service, and transparent family meals. Map nearby independents and chains in person before choosing a site; delivery directories are incomplete evidence.

## 3. Menu adaptations

**Recommendation (pilot menu, preferences unverified):** offer Jordanian spiced minced-beef and chicken-kebab pizzas; chicken or beef kebab sandwiches with pickles and garlic/tahini; and a cheese/vegetable pizza. Provide mild/medium/hot sauce choices. Test single-person and share-size pizzas, plus regular and larger sandwiches (with fries); set recipe weights/share counts after costing. Use Arabic/English descriptions. Abu Dhabi’s guide describes residents from 200+ nationalities, supporting multilingual descriptions, not a taste claim. [Residents Office](https://www.adro.gov.ae/Living-in-Abu-Dhabi/Where-To-Live)

## 4. Indicative AED price bands

**Observed menu anchors:** Talabat lists shawarma pizzas at AED 29 (Shawarma Planet; size unspecified) and AED 39.50 (Healthy Way; listing does not specify size). BBQ Shawarma lists small/large grilled kebab sandwiches at AED 14/19. Papa Johns lists an 8-inch meat pizza at AED 33 and a 13-inch at AED 69 on its UAE menu. These are listed item prices, not evidence of achieved sale prices or popularity. [Shawarma Planet](https://www.talabat.com/uae/restaurant/13803/shawarma-planet-al-maqta?aid=6485) · [Healthy Way](https://www.talabat.com/uae/restaurant/19868/healthy-way-restaurant-al-nahyan?aid=6485) · [BBQ Shawarma](https://www.talabat.com/uae/restaurant/772220/bbqshawarma-al-khalidiyah?aid=6485) · [Papa Johns UAE menu](https://www.papajohns.ae/menu)

**Planning bands to test, not market facts:** for pickup/dine-in, try a single-person 8-inch pizza at AED 30–36 and a 13-inch share pizza at AED 60–70; for aggregator listings, test AED 33–40 and AED 65–75 respectively. For one regular kebab wrap, test AED 14–19 pickup/dine-in and AED 16–24 on an aggregator. Portion dimensions anchor the pizza comparison to the cited menu; the wrap size is not standardized in competitor listings. App uplift is only a margin-protection hypothesis for negotiated platform charges, not a known fee rate. Confirm margins from recipe costing and written platform terms before launch.

## 5. Launch marketing

**Recommendation:** pilot bilingual videos, family bundle codes and review follow-up for 2–3 weeks. Seek sampling/meal-code partners among **Reem building managers/community groups**, **Khalifa City school parent groups** (with approval), and **sports venues such as Al Forsan or Abu Dhabi Cricket**. These are hypotheses, not confirmed relationships. The area guide verifies nearby schools and sports landmarks; partner access and conversion remain **evidence gaps**. [Residents Office area guide](https://www.adro.gov.ae/Living-in-Abu-Dhabi/Where-To-Live) Track acquisition cost, repeat orders and margin.

## 6. Delivery-platform strategy

**Fact:** Talabat’s UAE partner page says orders are dispatched through its app and rider network, delivery radius is based on a 15-minute drive, and merchants can pause orders; it requires business documents. Its commission amount is contract-specific and not disclosed on the public page. [Talabat UAE partner information](https://ae.partner.talabat.com/en)

**Recommendation:** onboard one major aggregator first, verify the actual service polygon and all fees in writing, use delivery-tested packaging, and add direct pickup/WhatsApp ordering for repeat customers where compliant. Do not assume the app’s radius equals practical coverage at peak traffic.

## 7. Licensing basics

**Fact:** ADDED’s official guide says determine activity and legal form, reserve trade name, obtain initial approval, secure a suitable location/tenancy contract, get any additional authority approvals, then obtain the business licence. Food premises should confirm current Abu Dhabi Agriculture and Food Safety Authority and municipality requirements; ADAFSA publishes a food-service premises design guideline on layout, equipment, cleanability and contamination prevention. [ADDED setup guide](https://added.gov.ae/en/set-up/establish-your-business) · [ADDED licensing requirements](https://www.added.gov.ae/en/set-up/establish-your-business/licensing-requirements) · [ADAFSA food-service design guideline](https://www.adafsa.gov.ae/CMS/Guidelines/Guideline%20No%20%286%29%20of%202019%20Food%20Service%20Design.pdf)

**Gap/action:** exact activity code, fit-out/layout approvals, inspection sequence and fees depend on site and legal form; obtain written TAMM/ADDED and ADAFSA guidance before signing a lease.

## 8. Operations and staffing

**Recommendation:** start with a small shift-based team covering grill/kebab, pizza/assembly, cashier/dispatch and a shift lead; cross-train prep and packing, and use order throttling to protect ticket times. This is a planning model, not a verified headcount. Talabat allows pausing orders when busy. UAE government guidance requires employers to register employees and secure work permits after licensing; permitted staffing quota and payroll costs need case-specific checks. [UAE government mainland recruiting guidance](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/business/doing-business-on-the-mainland/recruiting-on-the-mainland-) · [Talabat partner information](https://ae.partner.talabat.com/en)
