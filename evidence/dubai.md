# Dubai branch evidence

Checked 2026-09-23. **Facts** below are sourced; **recommendations/assumptions** are hypotheses to validate. No rent, footfall, sales, or popularity estimates are asserted.

## 1. Candidate areas and tradeoffs

- **JLT** — Fact: Visit Dubai describes a diverse dining scene and features an 11-stop food trail. **Recommendation:** shortlist for mixed dine-in and delivery, but expect meaningful restaurant competition; inspect a specific street/unit for access, delivery pickup, and direct rivals. [Visit Dubai: JLT](https://www.visitdubai.com/en/things-to-do/itineraries/jlt-foodie-trail)
- **Dubai Marina** — Fact: Visit Dubai highlights towers, family activities, and dining. **Recommendation:** test as a destination/residential catchment; tourist-facing visibility may come with higher occupancy cost, which needs broker quotes. [Visit Dubai: Marina](https://www.visitdubai.com/explore-dubai/dubai-neighbourhoods/dubai-marina)
- **Business Bay / JVC** — Planning alternatives to compare for residential/workday delivery. An older Visit Dubai guide characterizes Business Bay as close to Downtown dining and JVC as a residential community; it does not establish restaurant demand or current occupancy costs. [Visit Dubai guide](https://www.visitdubai.com/-/media/Files/study-dubai-guides/brochure-november-en-updated-03.pdf)

**Evidence gap:** current shop rents, permitted kitchen/ventilation suitability, catchment population, and order density. Obtain live comparable lease quotes and map delivery-app listings/order time windows before selecting a unit.

## 2. Competitors and positioning

- **Levantine street-food / grill:** Al Mallah identifies its roots in Lebanese food and fast takeaway, and lists shawarma, manakish, and grills. [Al Mallah](https://www.almallahuae.com/)
- **Pizza chains/value:** Papa Johns UAE lists classic and meat-heavy pizzas; its menu page shows Margherita at AED 29/45/62 (small/medium/large) and All The Meats at AED 33/50/69. [Papa Johns UAE menu](https://www.papajohns.ae/menu)
- **Premium casual pizza:** Pitfire’s delivery listing shows Margherita AED 60 (10-inch) / AED 80 (15-inch), and Pepperoni Primo AED 67 / AED 90. [Talabat: Pitfire Dubai Hills](https://www.talabat.com/uae/restaurant/708620/DUBAI-HILLS?aid=6946)

**Recommendation:** own the overlap—Jordanian-style loaded/meaty pizza plus kebab sandwiches, with clear portion/value choices. Named examples are category anchors, not a complete competitor or popularity ranking.

## 3. Menu adaptations

**Recommendation/assumption:** retain Jordanian signatures; offer familiar chicken/beef shawarma or kebab, garlic/tahini sauces, pickles, and vegetarian falafel/manakish sides. Al Mallah’s published range confirms local familiarity of shawarma, falafel, manakish, grilled meals, and mixed grills, but does not prove demand for this concept. [Al Mallah menu/order](https://order.almallahuae.com/)

Pilot mild and spicy profiles, individual and shareable sizes, and Arabic/English item descriptions. Confirm ingredient sourcing, halal documentation, allergens, and any nutrition-label obligations with the regulator before finalizing recipes.

## 4. AED price bands

**Observed menu facts (checked 2026-09-23):** Al Mallah direct order lists shawarma AED 10 regular / AED 17 saj and mixed-grill meal AED 42; Papa Johns lists small pizzas AED 29–33 and large AED 62–69; Pitfire examples span AED 60–90 by size/topping. [Al Mallah](https://order.almallahuae.com/) · [Papa Johns](https://www.papajohns.ae/menu) · [Pitfire on Talabat](https://www.talabat.com/uae/restaurant/708620/DUBAI-HILLS?aid=6946)

**Recommendation:** test kebab/wraps around AED 12–25, meal bundles AED 25–45, and meaty pizzas AED 35–75, with size and topping ladder. These are launch hypotheses anchored loosely to observed menu points, not verified competitor ranges or validated willingness-to-pay. Check dine-in and delivery prices separately and cost each recipe, packaging, tax/fees, and platform deductions first.

## 5. Marketing

**Hypothesis / launch sequence:** pre-opening, post Arabic/English dough-and-meat preparation clips and arrange tasting samples; soft-launch with a trackable opening bundle for nearby residents and offices; after the first weeks, shift spend toward the channels and menu items producing repeat orders. Test partnerships with nearby office and coworking communities for group lunch bundles and residential-community managers for tasting events; track redemptions and repeat purchases before expanding either.

**Compliance fact:** UAE Media Council says individuals advertising on social media generally need an Advertiser Permit, with an exemption for individuals promoting their own or their owned company’s products on personal accounts; paid creator campaigns should use eligible permitted creators and confirm current requirements. Dubai outdoor/signboard ads can also require permits. [UAE Media Council](https://uaemc.gov.ae/en/%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%AD-%D9%85%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%86/) · [Dubai advertising decree](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2020/Decree%20No.%20%286%29%20of%202020%20Regulating%20Advertisements%20in%20the%20Emirate%20of%20Dubai.html)

## 6. Delivery-platform strategy

**Fact:** Talabat’s partner page says its delivery radius is set within a 15-minute drive, requires business/trade-license and other applicable documents, and pays the first settlement 45 days after going live then every two weeks. Commission is charged, but no rate is published there; negotiate direct. [Talabat Partner UAE](https://ae.partner.talabat.com/en) Careem also provides restaurant partnership onboarding. [Careem partner FAQ](https://www.careem.com/en-AE/food-partner-faqs/)

**Recommendation:** launch on one high-reach marketplace plus direct pickup/ordering; add another only after measuring incremental orders, margin, prep load, and customer acquisition. Engineer pizza and wrap packaging for travel; pause orders when kitchen capacity is exceeded (Talabat supports pausing).

## 7. Licensing basics

**Fact:** Dubai Municipality requires food-related activity permits after food-code health conditions are met, and its service page says food-establishment design layout approval is needed before construction to activate the trade licence and start licensed food activity. Municipality publishes the Food Code, activity requirements, and food-related permit services. [Dubai Municipality services](https://www.dm.gov.ae/dubai-municipality-services/) · [Food traders and establishments](https://www.dm.gov.ae/municipality-business/food-traders-establishments/)

**Recommendation:** before lease/build-out, confirm the exact business activity and sequence with Dubai DET/licensing authority and Municipality; confirm layout, extraction/fire/building approvals, food permit, signage, tax registration applicability, and staff documentation with the relevant authorities. This is a checklist, not legal advice or an exhaustive permit determination.

## 8. Operations and staffing

**Fact:** Dubai Municipality describes a Person in Charge (PIC) as the food-safety/hygiene supervisor responsible for practices and staff; it lists an integrated training-management requirement for food establishments. [Municipality PIC overview](https://www.dm.gov.ae/dubai-municipality-oversees-more-than-70000-mandatory-pics-in-dubais-food-establishments/) · [Food establishment requirements](https://www.dm.gov.ae/municipality-business/food-traders-establishments/)

**Recommendation:** assign a trained PIC for each operating shift; cross-train pizza, grill, packing, and cashier roles, with separate checks for hot/cold holding, allergen communication, cleaning, stock rotation, and delivery handoff. Headcount and shift pattern are **evidence gaps** until service hours, seat count, menu throughput, and demand peaks are measured in a pilot.
